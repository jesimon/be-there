Be There

Be with the people you love, even from far away.

A simple screen in the home of someone who is housebound or lives alone, so family and friends can drop in and really be with them, not just call.

  • The person at home decides who may visit, and when.
  • They always see who is with them, and can end any visit with one tap.
  • Nothing is recorded.
