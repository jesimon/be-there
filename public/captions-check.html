Be There

Try captions on this device

During a visit, captions show in large letters what the visitor says. They are worked out on the device itself: no sound and no words leave it. They are a trial, in English only, and they make mistakes.

This page finds out whether this device is quick enough. Do it on the tablet that sits in their home, in the same browser and at the same address as its home screen. It also fetches what captions need, so the first visit does not have to wait for that.

1. Get ready

Fetches the speech model (about 49 MB the first time) and starts it.

2. Quick test

Writes down eleven seconds of clear speech, and times it.

3. Two-minute test

Plays that speech over and over, as if a visitor were talking, and shows captions for it just as a visit would. You will hear it. Keep this page open until it has finished.
